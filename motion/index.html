    How to read this page
    Monthly listeners
    How many different people played the artist on Spotify in the last 28 days. It's the fairest way to compare UG and mainstream acts, because every artist has it.
    🚀 In Motion · ▲ Moving · ● Still · ▼ Slowing
    How their listeners changed since the date shown on the card. In Motion means up 2% or more. Spotify rounds big numbers (Burna shows as "40.4M"), so small daily moves for big artists show as Still. Weekly changes appear once we have a week of data.
    Daily streams
    Total plays per day across all their songs, from kworb.net. Spotify doesn't publish this for smaller artists, so many UG acts show "not on the charts yet". That's normal, and it changes once they're big enough.
    Song streams
    Total plays of each song on Spotify and plays per day, from kworb.net. The arrow shows how daily plays changed since the date given. Available once an artist charts on kworb.
    UG → Mainstream motion
    A score out of 100: reach (listeners vs the smallest mainstream act we track, up to 50), features with mainstream stars (up to 30) and being on the daily stream charts (20). 0–39 Underground, 40–74 Breaking through, 75+ Crossed over.
